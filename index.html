Questions

A few things to know about Jobliox.

Who is Jobliox made for?

Jobliox is designed for independent service professionals and small service businesses that need a simpler way to manage customers, jobs, estimates, invoices, payments, and business records.

Does Jobliox include a free trial?

Yes. The planned Jobliox Pro subscription includes a 14-day full-access trial before a paid subscription is required.

What happens if I don't subscribe after the trial?

Jobliox is designed to move into read-only access after the trial if you do not subscribe. Your existing business records remain available to view, but creating or changing business records requires an active Pro subscription.

Can I create estimates and invoices?

Yes. Jobliox lets you create estimates and invoices, share professional PDFs, capture estimate acceptance, and convert approved estimates into invoices.

Can I track partial payments?

Yes. Jobliox supports recording full and partial payments, tracking remaining balances, and maintaining payment and refund history.

Can customers pay by card directly through Jobliox?

Direct card processing is not part of the initial Jobliox release. Jobliox currently focuses on recording and tracking payments made to your business.

Where will I be able to download Jobliox?

Jobliox is being prepared for iPhone. An official App Store download link will be added here when the app becomes available.
